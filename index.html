02. How We Use Collected Data

We process your data strictly to deliver user-facing group travel functionality:

  • Group Trip Facilitation: Displaying published itineraries, managing seat capacity (e.g., spots remaining), and issuing verified booking passes.
  • Identity Trust & Community Safety: Enforcing community standards, authenticating real humans, and protecting solo travelers from spam and fraudulent listings.
  • Instant Payout Operations: Routing 95% net disbursements to verified host bank accounts via Razorpay Route upon booking capture.
  • Automated Emergency Refunds: Executing immediate 100% traveler refunds if a host cancels or abandons a trip.
  • In-App Chat Coordination: Enabling private group chat rooms accessible exclusively by verified attendees who booked the same expedition.
Strict No-Sale Commitment: Roamio does NOT sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data, chat history, or contact information to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

04. Payment Processing & Escrow Split Policy

Roamio operates with 100% financial transparency under Reserve Bank of India (RBI) payment aggregator and nodal guidelines:

  • Zero Raw Financial Storage: Roamio never touches, views, or stores your credit card CVV, debit card PIN, net banking credentials, or UPI MPIN. All entries are captured inside Razorpay's PCI-DSS Level 1 certified checkout iframe/SDK.
  • 5% Platform Convenience Fee: A non-refundable 5% safety and platform convenience fee is added at checkout to support escrow routing, fraud monitoring, and platform infrastructure.
  • 95% Net Host Disbursement: The net 95% balance is transferred directly to the verified host's linked bank account/UPI ID via Razorpay Route upon booking capture to fund advance homestay and vehicle reservations.
  • 100% Emergency Host Cancellation Refund: If a host initiates an emergency trip cancellation, 100% of traveler fees are automatically refunded to their original payment source via Razorpay Route automated reversals (reverse_all: 1).

05. Identity & KYC Verification Safeguards

To ensure solo travelers—especially women and first-time explorers—feel completely safe on multi-day expeditions, hosts and travelers may verify their identity via Government ID (Aadhaar / Driving License / Voter ID):

  • Zero-Knowledge Verification: Identity checks are processed via cryptographically signed DigiLocker XML verification or authorized verification partners.
  • No Raw ID Storage: We do NOT store your 12-digit Aadhaar number, biometric data, or raw government ID photographs on our database servers.
  • Cryptographic Badges: Our system records solely a boolean verification flag (verified: true) and masked initials (e.g., "Liam V.") to grant trust badges.

06. Data Sharing & Intermediary Status Disclosure

In accordance with Section 79 of the Information Technology Act, 2000 and Rule 3(1)(a) of the IT Rules, 2021, Roamio operates strictly as an electronic marketplace facilitator connecting independent trip leaders with explorers.

  • Co-Traveler Visibility: When you book a seat, your display name, avatar, and trip chat communications are visible exclusively to other approved participants within that specific trip circle.
  • Host Applicant Review: Before booking confirmation, trip hosts view applicant profiles with masked identity details to confirm compatibility. Direct phone numbers are only shared after a booking is confirmed to facilitate airport/train pickup coordination.
  • Law Enforcement Requests: We do not disclose personal information to government authorities or third parties unless formally required under Indian criminal law, lawful court orders, or subpoenas.

07. Account & Data Deletion Mandate (Google Play Compliance)

Google Play requires all applications that allow account creation to provide an easily accessible pathway for users to permanently delete their account and associated data. Roamio provides both an in-app self-service mechanism and an external web request option:

Method 1: In-App Self-Service Deletion

  1. Open the Roamio mobile application.
  2. Tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the screen and tap "Delete Account".
  4. Type DELETE into the confirmation dialog and confirm. Your account, profile records, applications, and chat messages will be instantly deleted from our active database.

Method 2: External Web Deletion Request (Play Console Requirement)

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, you may submit a web deletion request directly to our developer team:

  • Email: mayurkale1444@gmail.com
  • Subject: Roamio Account & Data Deletion Request
  • Please send the request from the email address registered with your Roamio account.
  • Processing SLA: Requests are processed and permanently deleted from Cloud Firestore within 14 calendar days. You will receive an email confirmation once completed.
What is Deleted vs Retained: All personal profile data (name, email, avatar, bio, location, travel styles, chat messages, trip applications) will be permanently expunged. Financial payment reference logs are anonymized and retained for 7 years in cold storage strictly to satisfy Indian statutory tax and auditing requirements.

08. Data Retention Policies

  • Active Account Data: Retained while your account remains active.
  • Trip Chat Messages: Maintained for active trip coordination up to 90 days post-trip completion, after which message logs are automatically expunged.
  • Dispute Records: In the event of an active dispute or refund claim, records are retained until resolution by our mediation team.
  • Statutory Tax Records: Razorpay payment identifiers and invoice totals are retained for 7 years as mandated by the Indian Income Tax Department.

09. Security Safeguards & Encryption

We deploy defense-in-depth security measures to protect user data from unauthorized access, breach, or alteration:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the app, backend servers, and Firestore utilize Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • Encryption at Rest: All database documents and transaction ledgers stored in Cloud Firestore are encrypted using 256-bit Advanced Encryption Standard (AES-256).
  • Firestore Security Rules: Multi-layer zero-trust Firestore rules ensure users can only access their own private data and public trip listings.
  • Host Payout Isolation: Bank account numbers and IFSC codes stored for host payouts are encrypted with strict role-based access control.

10. Children's Privacy (Strictly 18+)

Roamio is strictly intended for adult explorers aged 18 years and older. We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or process personal data from children under the age of 18.

If we discover that a user under 18 has registered an account or reserved a seat without verified adult consent, we will terminate the account immediately and delete all corresponding data. To report an account belonging to a minor, contact us at mayurkale1444@gmail.com.

11. Android Permissions & Device Access

Roamio requests only the minimum device permissions necessary to function:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to fetch trip itineraries and sync real-time chat messages.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to detect offline state and provide graceful offline caching.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Optional): Used to alert travelers of host seat confirmations, departure reminders, and group chat replies. Can be toggled off anytime in Android settings.
  • android.permission.CAMERA / READ_MEDIA_IMAGES (Optional): Requested only when uploading an avatar or trip cover photograph.
